# Repair by alternating cycles or by component states?

Protect two of the four rounds. Their triples are the two sides of a cubic bipartite graph, and each original variable is the edge joining the two triples that contain it. A coloring selects a set of red edges. Flipping the edges of a red/blue alternating cycle preserves each protected triple's number of red vertices.

For a fixed pair of rounds this invariant is complete if other rounds may be violated along the path. Given two edge sets with the same degrees on both sides, orient the edges present only in the first from left to right, and those present only in the second from right to left. Every vertex has equal in- and out-degree. Removing directed simple cycles decomposes the difference into alternating cycles, and flipping them successively realizes the endpoint. When the protected degrees are all one, a starting matching is computable by bipartite matching. Reaching a four-round proper endpoint in that degree profile would find an exact transversal, since the total number of selected edges equals the number of triples in each round. That endpoint problem is already NP-complete. [Alternating-cycle and exact-transversal antecedents](../../RELATED-WORK.md)

## Two violations and the correct total weight can still trap every pair choice

In the [fifteen-point cyclic construction](IMBALANCE.md) with $k\ge2$ use physical masks 1676 and 2989 (hexadecimal) in the first two blocks and 3263 in the others. All three unchanged rounds are proper. Exactly two triples in the affected round are monochromatic. Both are copies of the internal triple (0,11,13). Every link is proper. The first two blocks have weights eight and six, and the others have weight seven. The total $7k$ equals the weight of a full solution.

Each individual block is a union of complete triples in each of the three unchanged rounds. Any chosen pair of rounds contains at least one of these three. An alternating-cycle move preserves the red count in each triple of that protected round, hence in the entire block. This holds when the protected pair changes between moves and even when violations are permitted in other rounds. Neither of the two globally proper colorings has the starting block-weight vector. No such walk reaches a solution. [Executable starts and original-clause checks](../../test_phase_repair.py)

## A polynomial case that the cycle walk misses

Given the four rounds, omit one when finding connected components. Reinsert every omitted triple wholly inside a component into its local formula. For a component of $s$ vertices, enumerate its $2^s$ assignments. An empty table certifies inconsistency. On its boundary vertices test whether every local model restricts to a fixed bit pattern or its complement. This condition allows many different models on interior vertices. NAE symmetry ensures that a reference local witness and its complement realize both boundary states.

Use one phase bit per component. A cross triple meeting two components sees two ports of the same component. If their reference colors differ, it is satisfied for either phase. If they agree, its other port must disagree, imposing a binary parity equation between phases. A graph traversal solves all these equations or finds a contradiction. Expanding the phases gives a checked coloring of the original triples. When a boundary requires more than these two states, a triple meets three different components, or the configured local table budget is exceeded, the implementation can return UNKNOWN.

The algorithm is polynomial on inputs with logarithmic component size, two complementary boundary states and cross triples meeting at most two components. In the trapped family above it recovers the two legal phases from the fifteen-vertex components and solves their equality cycle. A different starting assignment is unnecessary. This API takes the round decomposition explicitly. [Implementation](../../phase_repair.py)
